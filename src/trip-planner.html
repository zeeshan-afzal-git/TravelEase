Trip Planning Guide

Get ready for your journey with practical tips and resources

🚗 Transport Tips

  • Use ride-sharing apps for convenience and safety.
  • Book intercity buses in advance during holiday seasons.
  • Carry small change for local transport fares.

🏨 Stay Tips

  • Look for guest reviews on Booking.com or Airbnb.
  • Stay close to central locations to save transport costs.
  • Confirm check-in and check-out times before arrival.

💰 Budgeting

  • Plan a daily spending limit and track your expenses.
  • Exchange currency at reputed banks or booths.
  • Use budgeting apps like Trail Wallet or Splitwise.

🎒 Packing Essentials

  • Pack light — use layers instead of heavy clothing.
  • Bring a universal adapter and power bank.
  • Keep photocopies of your passport and IDs.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is it safe to travel solo?

A: Yes, but always stay alert and follow local guidelines.

Q: Do I need travel insurance?

A: Highly recommended, especially for international trips.

Q: How can I manage connectivity?

A: Buy a local SIM card or use a global roaming plan.
