Explore Pakistan

Discover the beauty of Gilgit-Baltistan & Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Gilgit-Baltistan

Description: A mountainous paradise offering breathtaking landscapes and peace.

Major Attractions: Hunza Valley, Skardu, Fairy Meadows, Khunjerab Pass

Best Time to Visit: May to October

Culture & Food: Wakhi, Balti cultures; local dishes include Gyako, Mamtu

Estimated Travel Cost: 30,000 PKR - 70,000 PKR (7-day trip)

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Description: Known for its lush green valleys, history, and hospitality.

Major Attractions: Swat Valley, Malam Jabba, Kalam, Mahodand Lake

Best Time to Visit: April to September

Culture & Food: Pashtun culture; dishes include Chapli Kebab, Kabuli Pulao

Estimated Travel Cost: 25,000 PKR - 60,000 PKR (5–7 day trip)
